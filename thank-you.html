How to begin

  • Print on 8.5 × 11 paper with the scale set to “Fit” (sometimes called “Fit to page” or “Fit to printable area”) — not “Actual size” — so nothing gets cut off at the edges.
  • Each evening, write one success and one thing to improve on your Daily Reflection.
  • On the tracker, mark your level each morning and evening.
  • Fill in the circles below each day — small for a little, large for a lot — and jot a few words in Notes.
  • When a sheet is full, hang it on the wall and line the next one up beside it using the small tick marks on the edges. Watch your line grow.
  • On the hard days, look down the column. The inputs tell the story — meet yourself there with compassion.
