About Alexa

A fellow
traveler,
not a teacher.

The State of Creation is a physical state of being where we tap into the energy needed to create our lives from a place of courage.
I created this concept because I believe the courage it takes to face resistance on the journey to our next phase serves a purpose: it is what makes us strong enough to get there. There is so much momentum and energy in consistent compounding action. We simply need to become conscious enough to realize that the resistance is an opportunity for growth.

I am with you. We are here together. Let's get to work.
