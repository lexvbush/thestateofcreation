The State of Creation is an embodied state of high-frequency energy where purpose, courage, and potential are naturally accessible.

I created this concept because I believe that the courage and strength needed to overcome the resistance we experience in life serves a deeper purpose: it is the opportunity to access the embodiment of our potential selves.

As the butterfly becomes ready to release herself from the chrysalis, the strength she develops in her wings while she is breaking free is what allows her to fly.

Momentum is gained in consistent compounding action. We simply need to become conscious enough to realize that the resistance we are facing moment to moment is the opportunity for the growth we need to fly.

We are in this together. Let's get to work.
